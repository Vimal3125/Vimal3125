What to do in the first hour

  1. Don't reply and don't delete anything yet.
  2. Take screenshots showing the username, date/time and the message (use the Evidence Log tab).
  3. Block and report the account on the platform.
  4. Tell someone you trust. You don't have to handle this alone.
  5. File a complaint on the portal or at your nearest police station / cyber cell.

Quick guides

    
Someone is threatening to leak my photos
      
Do not pay or send more content. Save the threats as evidence, report the account, and use StopNCII.org for intimate images. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 / 112. Blackmailers rely on your silence, so telling a trusted person helps.

      

    
Someone made a fake profile of me
      
Collect the profile link and screenshots. Use the platform's "impersonation" report option, ask friends to report it too, and include it in your cybercrime complaint.

      

    
I'm being stalked or flooded with messages
      
Block all channels, keep a dated log, set accounts to private, turn off location sharing, and tell someone who can check in on you. Repeated contact after you said stop is reportable.

      

    
I'm receiving abusive comments / trolling
      
You don't owe anyone a reply. Use "restrict", "mute" and comment filters, report in bulk, and screenshot first. Persistent targeted abuse can be reported to the police.

      

    
I want to support a friend who is being harassed
      
Listen without blaming. Never ask "why did you share that?". Help her gather evidence, report accounts, and go with her to file the complaint if she wants.
